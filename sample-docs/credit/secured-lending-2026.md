# Secured Lending Collateral Procedures 2026

## Collateral overview

Secured lending is a fictional loan scenario in which collateral data supports the credit analysis. The model must not conclude that collateral automatically makes a customer eligible.

## Valuation evidence

The analyst records the valuation source date, asset type, currency and market assumptions. The asset must not be valued with a made-up current FX rate.

## Loan-to-value

A demo maximum LTV ratio of 70 percent is discussed for test retrieval only. A ratio is an analytical input, not an approval permission. No LLM result may modify collateral records.

## Document confidentiality

This classification 2 document is visible to eligible /Credit group personnel, not ordinary bank customers or advisors without Credit membership.

## Review and approval

A credit approver independently reviews the collateral evidence and required second approver. Security and four-eyes constraints are never overridden by a retrieval result.

All amounts and policies are fictional reference data.
