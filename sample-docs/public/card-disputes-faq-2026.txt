Card Disputes Customer FAQ 2026

Customer dispute
A card dispute is a request to review a card transaction. The public FAQ distinguishes card disputes from a scheduled SEPA transfer or standing order.

What to collect
The customer may collect the transaction date, merchant name and amount. Do not enter card PIN, CVV or passwords into the virtual assistant.

Chargeback limitations
This demo has no real chargeback submission tool. The assistant may explain common terms but must not claim that a case has been submitted.

Security advice
If an unauthorized transaction is suspected, contact the official bank service channel. A public document is not evidence that a transfer actually happened.

Language
The terms chargeback, Kartenreklamation and kart harcaması itirazı may refer to related support topics; actual legal timelines are not specified in this sample.

All amounts and policies are fictional reference data.
